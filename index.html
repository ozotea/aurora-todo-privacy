Privacy Policy — Aurora ToDo

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Aurora ToDo ("the app") is a task-planning app for Android. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Summary

Aurora ToDo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data storage

All tasks, categories, and settings you create are stored locally on your device in a private, app-only database. This data is never sent to us or to any third party, because the app has no server and no internet access.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — used only to show task reminders (15 minutes before and at the start of a task) and a daily summary notification. No notification content leaves your device.
  • Schedule exact alarms — used only to trigger the reminder notifications above at the correct time.
  • Receive boot completed — used only to reschedule your reminders after your device restarts, so existing reminders keep working.

None of these permissions are used to collect, read, or transmit data about you.

Backup and restore

The app lets you manually export your tasks to a JSON file of your choosing, and import one back later. This is entirely user-initiated: the file is written to or read from storage you select, and is never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other purpose. No data is shared with third parties because none is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that requires data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
